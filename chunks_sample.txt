That the add/drop deadline and the withdrawal deadline are different dates and only one of them is on the calendar everyone reads.

That you can take a course pass/fail and declare it late ù up to week eight. I carried a grade I didn't need to.

--- reply 5 (17 votes) ---
That your adviser's job is partly to know the exceptions to rules. Ask before assuming a deadline is fixed.

Document early. Not to be difficult ù because if you go to the instructor in week 10 with nothing written down, there's nothing they can do.

--- reply 1 (24 votes) ---
For a course outside your major that you're taking because you're curious. That's what it's for and most people never use it.

--- reply 2 (38 votes) ---
The part that isn't advertised: you can declare it as late as week eight. So take the midterm first, then decide.

--- reply 4 (12 votes) ---
Two per year and eight across the degree. I hit the annual limit in second year and regretted spending one on an easy course.

THREAD: First winter here ù what do I need?

--- reply 1 (26 votes) ---
Layers, not a big coat. The buildings are overheated and you'll carry a parka around all day.

Boots with actual tread. The path past the pond ices over and people go down on it every year.

Get a light that clips to your bag. It's dark by 4:30 in December and parts of the walk from Fenwick aren't lit well.
